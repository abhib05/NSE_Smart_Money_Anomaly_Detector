5. Limitations

  • Corporate actions come from NSE's free-text announcements; splits/bonuses missing from that list are inferred from the exchange's adjusted previous close, and unexplained jumps are excluded.
  • No point-in-time sector classification is available from NSE archives, so the 30% sector cap is not applied.
  • Weekend special sessions (Muhurat trading, Budget-day Saturdays) are not ingested.
  • Execution at the next open assumes fills at the official open price; slippage is modelled, but queue position and auction dynamics are not.
  • Delisted holdings exit at the last price minus a 10% haircut; labels for delisted stocks use the last available price.
  • The Nifty 500 benchmark is a price index (no dividends).
  • Costs use 2025-26 published rates; rates have changed over the study period.
  • Daily ICs of overlapping multi-day labels are autocorrelated; the block bootstrap mitigates but does not remove this.
